## 1. Why This Product Exists

FieldPulse Solutions sells field-service-management software to HVAC and plumbing contractors. Their customers' technicians today operate on an SMS-plus-paper workflow that delays billing by roughly two days and has become an active competitive liability — nine customer businesses have left FieldPulse over the last twelve months explicitly citing the lack of mobile capability, and the rate is accelerating. PulseField Mobile is the mobile application that puts full job context in technicians' hands and feeds completed work back to the FieldPulse platform the same day. It exists to defend FieldPulse's customer base against mobile-capable competitors (ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro) and to re-establish FieldPulse as a credible choice for new customers — *before* the competitor sales push that historically peaks in Q4.

---

## 2. Who It's For

**Primary users:** **HVAC and plumbing field-service technicians** employed by FieldPulse's ~80 customer businesses. Estimated ~950 active technicians across the customer base. They work in residential and commercial premises — basements, crawlspaces, mechanical rooms, new-construction sites — frequently without network connectivity. Most are non-technical users for whom the app must be intuitive enough to learn on the job. Year-1 adoption target: 30–40% of the active population.

**Secondary users:** **Office dispatchers** at FieldPulse customer businesses — the people sending today's SMS dispatches and processing today's paper forms. Almost every FieldPulse customer has at least one. They continue to operate through the existing FieldPulse Web Platform; they benefit from real-time visibility into technician status and faster job-completion data.

**Indirect stakeholders:** **FieldPulse Solutions leadership** (Marcus Chen, Priya Krishnan), who own the customer-retention story; and **FieldPulse's customer businesses** themselves, whose billing cycles compress when the app works.

---

## 3. What Problem It Solves

Today, a technician dispatched to a job receives an SMS with limited detail, phones the office for full context, performs the work, captures notes and signatures on paper, and returns the paper to the office at end of day for next-morning data entry — leading to a two-day lag before invoicing. This wastes time per job, blocks same-day billing, and signals to customers that FieldPulse is a generation behind competitors who already offer mobile. PulseField Mobile collapses that workflow into a single application that delivers job context, captures completion data on-site (offline-capable), and synchronises to the platform when connectivity returns.

---

## 4. What Success Looks Like

- **≥20 of FieldPulse's existing customer businesses** have technicians actively using PulseField Mobile within six months of go-live.
- **Billing cycle compressed from ~2 days to same-day** for jobs handled fully through the app — the headline metric Marcus's customers care about.
- **Mobile becomes a positive acquisition factor** in new-customer conversations within twelve months — moving from "reason customers don't leave" to "reason customers choose FieldPulse".
- **Technician daily-active-use rate ≥30% of installed base by month three** — the early-warning floor below which something is wrong.
- **FieldPulse's parallel SOC 2 readiness work completes** without PulseField Mobile creating compliance barriers.

---

## 5. Why Us / Why Now

Customer churn citing mobile is concrete and accelerating: six customers in the last calendar year, three more in Q1 2026 alone. Competitors push hardest in Q4 going into the new year — the goal is to have something credible in technicians' hands before that push, even if the product is right-sized rather than feature-complete. FieldPulse owns the field-service-management workflow already; PulseField Mobile is an additive surface — not a platform reset, not a vertical expansion, not a rewrite. A focused mobile app that respects the existing FieldPulse Web Platform integration and the team's small-company scale ($180K–$250K envelope, 12-person organisation, no in-house mobile team yet) defends the customer base without overcommitting.

---

## 6. What This Product Is Not

- **Not a customer-facing portal.** FieldPulse customers' end-customers are out of scope — this is a tool for the technician and dispatcher workflow, not for the homeowner or property manager being served.
- **Not a redesign or rewrite of the existing FieldPulse Web Platform.** The mobile app is additive — it integrates with what's there, it doesn't replace it.
- **Not an expansion beyond HVAC and plumbing.** Other FSM verticals (electrical, landscaping, cleaning) are deliberately out of scope.
- **Not a SOC 2 implementation project.** SOC 2 work runs in parallel and PulseField Mobile must not block it — but Vision-level success is measured on the mobile product, not on certification.
