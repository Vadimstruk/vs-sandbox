# Workflows Reference

easyterms-agents includes two workflows:

---

## Create Business Requirement

**Workflow:** `create-business-requirement`  
**Entry:** `workflows/create-business-requirement/workflow.md`

**Purpose:** Guide Business Analysts through a structured 7-step process to create validated, business-only requirements with full context, ready for developer handoff and Jira insertion.

**When to Use:** When you need a single, well-scoped business requirement with clear value, rules, patterns, and regulatory context.

**Key Steps:**

1. Load context
2. Identify value
3. Explore rules
4. Check patterns
5. Validate regulatory
6. Document requirement
7. Confirm / generate Jira

**Agent(s):** Business Analyst (Sarah)

---

## Lightweight Brainstorming

**Workflow:** `lightweight-brainstorming`  
**Entry:** `workflows/lightweight-brainstorming/workflow.md`

**Purpose:** Facilitate quick business ideation (15–20 minutes) focused on business needs, user value, and regulatory considerations, producing a structured ideation document ready for the Create Business Requirement workflow.

**When to Use:** When you want to explore ideas or options before committing to a full requirement.

**Key Steps:**

1. Init
2. Generate ideas
3. Categorize / prioritize
4. Prepare for BR

**Agent(s):** Business Analyst (Sarah)
